_Hate unwittingly has a colour._  
_It changes, to mask what one lacks._  
_If you see hate only without, you ignore it within._  
_Carry your hate on your shoulders,_  
_in big unwieldy sacks._  
_Perhaps then you'll see it weighs you down,_  
_Or allow rationale to seep-in._  
_The colour of hate changes, from violet to red,_  
_Perhaps to you it is green,_  
_But for others saffron is your spleen._
